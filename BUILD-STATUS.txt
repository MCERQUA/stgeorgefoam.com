SIBLING-SITE AWARENESS (important context for this build)
Discovered mid-build that /workspace/Websites/stgeorgesprayfoam.com already exists — a
fully-built, live-gated CCA site for the SAME city/niche (built 2026-07-31), positioned as
a narrow "spray-foam-only specialist, residential only, nothing else" company (dark
canyon-navy + teal palette). A second sibling, southernutahinsulation.com, is a general
insulation contractor (light terracotta + amber palette) covering St. George + Cedar City.

To avoid duplicate content / duplicate positioning across two live CCA domains in the same
market, stgeorgefoam.com was deliberately differentiated:
- POSITIONING: full-service spray foam company — residential AND commercial/metal-building
  work, new installs AND spray foam removal/correction for jobs other contractors got
  wrong. Still 100% spray-foam-material-exclusive (never fiberglass/batts/blown-in) — just
  not narrow in scope. Two of the 8 services (Commercial & Metal Building Spray Foam, and
  Spray Foam Removal & Correction) are NOT offered by the sibling site.
- DESIGN: distinct "Desert Copper & Sage" light palette (copper #B5502E / sage #7A8B6F /
  sand #FAF6EF / charcoal #2A211B) — verified via the pre-deploy-content-check palette
  scanner as containing zero purple/pink/blue-gradient hues, and visually distinct from
  both siblings' palettes (hex-by-hex compared before choosing).
- CONTENT: all services/blog/FAQ copy independently authored (not reused from the sibling),
  verified via grep sweep to contain zero mentions of the sibling's brand name/domain.

and (d) given entirely new service/blog/FAQ content (see CONTENT below). This is a
copy-editing/re-theme starting point, not a content or design clone — see grep-sweep
verification below.
