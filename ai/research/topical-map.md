## 2. Service Pages (8 — spray foam only, no exceptions)

Each page: hot-desert-specific angle, all reinforcing the specialist positioning line.

### 2.1 `/services/open-cell-spray-foam`
**Open-Cell Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: The economical air-sealing option for interior wall cavities and attic underside applications where moisture control is less critical than air-sealing — stops hot desert air infiltration and sound transmission. In Washington County's dry climate, open-cell's lower moisture resistance is rarely a liability (unlike humid markets), making it a cost-effective choice for interior partition walls and non-roof-deck attic work. Covers R-value per inch (~3.5–3.7/inch), expansion behavior, and where it's the right call vs. closed-cell.

### 2.2 `/services/closed-cell-spray-foam`
**Closed-Cell Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: The high-density, high-R-value-per-inch (~6–7/inch) option for roof decks, rim joists, and exterior-adjacent assemblies taking direct desert heat load — plus structural rigidity for stem walls and monsoon-driven moisture intrusion points. Positioned as the premium choice for St. George's most heat-punished building assemblies (west-facing walls, roof decks, garage ceilings under direct sun).

### 2.3 `/services/attic-spray-foam-insulation` — FLAGSHIP DESERT-CLIMATE PAGE
**Attic & Roof-Deck Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: The single highest-impact page on the site. Direct roof-deck spray foam application converts a vented attic into a sealed, conditioned "unvented attic" assembly — stopping the 130–150°F+ hot-roof-deck heat radiating straight into HVAC ductwork and ceiling drywall. Explicitly frames the "hot roof deck problem": St. George attics without roof-deck foam routinely become second furnaces every May–September, forcing AC systems (and ductwork sitting in that superheated space) to fight radiant heat gain non-stop. This page should also address the radiant-barrier-adjacent conversation (foam vs. reflective barrier — foam wins on total assembly performance) and tie directly to lower Rocky Mountain Power / Dixie Power summer bills. This is the page most likely to carry the primary "spray foam St George" commercial keyword intent.

### 2.4 `/services/wall-spray-foam-insulation`
**Wall Cavity Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: Exterior wall assemblies — especially west- and south-facing walls that take the brunt of afternoon desert sun — lose the fight against heat gain with old fiberglass batts that have gaps, compression, or settling. Closed-cell or open-cell wall application stops both conductive and convective heat transfer through exterior walls, addressing the "why does my west-facing bedroom stay hot all evening" complaint common in Washington County homes.

### 2.5 `/services/crawl-space-spray-foam`
**Crawl Space Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: Encapsulating and foaming crawl space walls/rim areas in Washington County homes stops both ground-radiated summer heat and monsoon-season moisture intrusion (flash-flood-adjacent grading issues are common after summer storms). Distinct from the sibling site's general "crawl space insulation/encapsulation" service — this page is spray-foam-application only, not vapor-barrier-and-batt work.

### 2.6 `/services/garage-spray-foam-insulation`
**Garage Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: Detached and attached garages in St. George/Hurricane/Washington City routinely exceed 120–130°F in summer, radiating heat into adjacent living space and turning garages into unusable storage-only space. Spray foam on garage ceilings (especially under direct-sun roof decks) and walls solves both problems — usable garage space and reduced heat transfer into the home.

### 2.7 `/services/new-construction-spray-foam`
**New Construction Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: Builder/GC-facing page for St. George's active new-home growth corridor (Washington Fields, Little Valley, Desert Color, Hurricane's growing subdivisions). Positions spray-foam-only pre-drywall installation as the way to hit or exceed 2021 IECC Zone 3B code minimums efficiently in one trade visit, with tighter envelopes than batt/blown-in alone. Coordination with framers and HVAC rough-in emphasized.

### 2.8 `/services/rim-joist-band-joist-spray-foam`
**Rim Joist / Band Joist Spray Foam Insulation**
Angle: The rim joist band around the foundation perimeter is a chronic air-leak point even in slab-on-grade-heavy Washington County construction (basements and crawl spaces where present). Small-footprint, high-value spray foam application that stops both hot-air infiltration in summer and dust/monsoon-driven debris infiltration at this seam — an easy upsell/add-on to attic or crawl-space jobs.
