## Name: Desert Copper & Sage

Distinct from both sibling St. George spray-foam sites already in the fleet:
- `southernutahinsulation.com` — light terracotta + amber + warm-cream (general insulation contractor)
- `stgeorgesprayfoam.com` — dark canyon-navy + teal + rust (spray-foam-only specialist)

This site (`stgeorgefoam.com`) is light, warm, and full-service — burnt copper primary with a
desert-sage secondary accent, distinct hue mix from both siblings.

## Motif
Full-service positioning (residential + commercial + correction/removal) — sun-baked desert
professionalism, copper accent lines, rounded-12 cards, warm soft shadows. Sage inverted band
used for the "why full-service" differentiator section and footer.

## Brand positioning (differentiator from sibling)
St. George Foam is the Washington County **full-service** spray foam company: residential AND
commercial/metal-building work, new installs AND spray foam removal/correction for jobs other
crews got wrong. The sibling site is a narrow spray-foam-only residential specialist with no
commercial or correction offering — this site deliberately covers that gap.
